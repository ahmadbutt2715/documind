About DocuMind

Make your documents feel within reach.

Important knowledge should not disappear into a folder. DocuMind gives you a direct, conversational way to understand the documents your work depends on.

The idea

Ask better questions of the information you already have.

Built for clarity

Why it exists

Less hunting. More understanding.

We built DocuMind around a simple observation: the answer is often already in the room, buried in a brief, a report, or a set of notes.

Instead of making you remember the exact filename or phrase, DocuMind lets you start with the question. It brings the relevant context forward so you can move from reading to doing with confidence.

Our principles

A calmer way to work with knowledge.

01

Context first

Useful answers begin with the right passages, not a guess dressed up as certainty.

02

Plain language

Your questions do not need to sound technical. Ask naturally and keep your attention on the work.

03

Useful by design

Every interaction should help you make a decision, find a detail, or take the next step.
